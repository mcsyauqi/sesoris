1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

Key findings:
- Indonesia has 157M+ active TikTok users; Gen Z uses TikTok/YouTube for
  product discovery. Video-first SEO is critical.
- "Rumah minimalis" cluster has massive volume (90,500/mo) but is mostly
  architecture-focused. Target the sub-niche: organization within minimalist homes.
- Kitchen storage ("rak dapur", "rak piring", "kitchen set minimalis") is a
  strong category with high search volume and low keyword difficulty.
- Storage box/organizer keywords have moderate volume with VERY low competition
  (KD 0 on most terms) -- huge opportunity.
- Ramadan/Lebaran (Feb-Apr) and Harbolnas (11.11/12.12) are peak seasonal moments.
- "Warm minimalism" and "sustainable storage" (bamboo, rattan) are 2026 trends.
- Travel organizer demand peaks in summer (Jul-Aug) and holiday seasons.
- The KonMari method has 94M TikTok views; decluttering content consistently trends.

7. COMPETITOR KEYWORD GAPS & OPPORTUNITIES
================================================================================

Key competitors in Indonesia home organization space:
- AZKO (azko.id) / Ruparupa -- 600+ stores, massive content strategy
- Dekoruma (dekoruma.com) -- Japandi focused, custom interior
- IKEA Indonesia (ikea.co.id) -- brand keyword dominance
- Informa -- offline retail + online
- Homedoki (homedoki.id) -- niche home organizer
- OneSo (oneso.id) -- storage & organization niche

GAPS TO EXPLOIT (competitors weak here):
1. Indonesian-language "how-to" content for organization (cara menata...)
2. Specific room-type organization guides (dapur sempit, kamar kost, rumah subsidi)
3. Product comparison content (rak piring stainless vs plastik, etc.)
4. Seasonal content (Lebaran prep, Ramadan organization)
5. KonMari / decluttering content in Bahasa Indonesia
6. Travel organization niche (packing cubes, cable organizers)
7. "Aesthetic" organization content targeting Gen Z
8. Video-first content (TikTok SEO, YouTube Shorts)

BRAND KEYWORDS YOUR COMPETITORS OWN (do NOT target these):
- storage box ikea (390/mo)
- storage box mr diy (480/mo)
- rak dapur ikea (210/mo)
- storage box informa (90/mo)
- kotak penyimpanan ikea (90/mo)
