# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

US households, mostly renters and first-home owners aged 25-45, shopping on a phone in the evening or on a laptop on a weekend. They have one specific problem space in mind (the under-sink cabinet, a deep base cabinet, a closet floor full of shoes, a makeup drawer, a suitcase) and a tape measure nearby. Many arrive from a how-to article on the blog before they ever see a product. The job: find an organizer that physically fits the space, trust the store enough to pay with PayPal, and move on with their day.

## Product Purpose

Sesoris (sesoris.com) is an independent online store for home organization: pull-out cabinet organizers, pantry and over-the-door racks, shoe storage, storage bins, makeup and travel organizers. Products ship from a US warehouse (free shipping over $50, flat $5.99 below), checkout runs on PayPal. A daily-published blog of 600+ organization guides is the main acquisition channel. Success = a visitor who lands on any page understands what Sesoris sells within one screen, can tell whether a product fits their space, and completes checkout.

## Brand Personality

Measured, tidy, quietly satisfying. The feeling of a drawer gliding out on ball-bearing rails and every item being reachable. Voice is practical and specific (inches, pounds, tiers, what's in the box), never hype. Confident without claiming things the store cannot prove: no invented ratings, customer counts, or "24/7" promises.

## Anti-references

- Generic 2019 Shopify theme: green-tinted stock-photo hero with a glass stat card, four icon trust badges, pill tabs, a newsletter band with a dotted pattern. This is what the site looked like before the 2026-10 redesign.
- Marketplace clutter (Temu, AliExpress): countdown timers, red discount stickers on everything, fake scarcity.
- Cream-and-serif "editorial home goods" lane (warm paper backgrounds, italic serif headlines, mono labels).
- Glassmorphism, gradient text, numbered section eyebrows on every section.

## Design Principles

1. Fit before feelings. Dimensions, capacity, and what's in the box are first-class content, not fine print.
2. Product is the hero. Real product photography on calm tiles beats mood stock photos.
3. Every claim is true. Shipping, returns, and support copy must match `src/lib/checkout.ts` and the policy pages.
4. Content and commerce are one site. The blog and the shop share the same type, color, and components so readers who arrive from an article feel they are in the store.
5. Works one-handed on a phone. Add to cart, filters, and navigation are reachable without hover.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: body text at least 4.5:1, touch targets at least 24x24 (44 preferred), visible focus rings, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, no hover-only actions.
